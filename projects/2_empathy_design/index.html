Empathy-Centric Design

Tools and methods for embedding empathy into participatory design, policy-making, and large-scale civic applications.

Designing technology for societal challenges requires more than functional requirements — it requires understanding the perspectives, experiences, and emotions of the people affected. My work in this area develops empathy-centric frameworks that bridge the gap between end-users and decision-makers.

COCTEAU

The COCTEAU (Co-Creating The European Union) tool (Mauri et al., 2022) is an empathy-based platform for participatory decision-making. It uses creative scenarios and role-play mechanics to help citizens and policymakers explore policy options from diverse perspectives.

COCTEAU has been applied to policy challenges in healthcare, housing, and urban planning contexts, and was validated in the context of the EU Horizon project.

EmpathiCH Workshops

I have co-organized the EmpathiCH workshop series at CHI — one of the most competitive venues in HCI — over three consecutive years:

  • EmpathiCH @ CHI 2022 (Mauri et al., 2022)
  • EmpathiCH @ CHI 2023 (Drouet et al., 2023)
  • EmpathiCH @ CHI 2024 (Debnath et al., 2024)

Policy Sandboxing

The Policy Sandboxing framework (Mauri et al., 2024) uses empathy as an enabler for inclusive policy-making, allowing stakeholders to safely explore the impact of policy decisions before implementation. Published in CSCW 2024.

AI for Local Communities

In collaboration with Carnegie Mellon University and TU Delft, I explored how AI tools can empower local communities in civic engagement and environmental monitoring (Hsu et al., 2022).
