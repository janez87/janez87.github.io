Large-scale data management cannot rely on machines alone. Human judgment is essential for handling ambiguity, ensuring quality, and interpreting domain-specific context. My research develops **human-in-the-loop frameworks** that integrate crowd contributions intelligently at each stage of the data pipeline.

### Data collection and quality

I have developed models and tools for evaluating and improving data quality in crowdsourced settings, including crowd-based annotation tasks and quality metrics that account for worker expertise and bias.

### Crowdsourcing platforms and patterns

Early work at Politecnico di Milano produced pattern-based specifications for crowdsourcing applications, community-based crowdsourcing models, and adaptive platform designs.

### Applications

Human-in-the-loop methods have been applied across several domains:

- **Music transcription** — microtask crowdsourcing for music score error detection.
- **Energy consumption** — user-generated content analysis for smart city energy behavior.
- **Social research** — complementing studies on vulnerable youth with social media data.
- **Privacy** — human-in-the-loop approaches to image privacy preservation.
