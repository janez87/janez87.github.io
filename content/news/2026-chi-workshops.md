Two workshop papers accepted at **CHI 2026** (Barcelona):

- *Co-Data: Cultivating Effective Human-LLM Collaboration for Collaborative Data Processing* — with Amedeo Pachera, Kashif Imteyaz, Jie Yang, Eric Umuhoza, Angela Bonifati, Michal Lahav, and Nitesh Goyal.
- *(Re-) Thinking Empathy's Materiality in HCI* — with Sophia Ppali, Mireia Yurrita, Alice Vitali, Alok Debnath, Lucie Flek, Andrea Cuadra, Sven Mayer, Michal Lahav, Tiffanie Horne, and Aneesha Singh.
