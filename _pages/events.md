## Workshops Organized

**EmpathiCH: Scrutinizing Empathy-Centric Design Beyond the Individual**
CHI 2024 · [Website](https://empathich.github.io/2024/)
Third iteration of the EmpathiCH workshop series, reassessing HCI's focus on empathy in design beyond individual experiences.

---

**EmpathiCH: Unraveling Empathy-Centric Design**
CHI 2023 · [Website](https://empathich.github.io/2023/)
One-day interactive workshop exploring empathy frameworks and tools for human-centered design at scale.

---

**Empathy-Centric Design at Scale**
CHI 2022 · [Website](https://empathich.github.io/2022/)
First edition of the EmpathiCH series, bringing together scholars to discuss empathy for large-scale societal impact.
