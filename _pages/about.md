I am a Junior Professor (*Maître de Conférences*) at [Université Claude Bernard Lyon 1](https://www.univ-lyon1.fr), affiliated with the [LIRIS Research Lab](https://liris.cnrs.fr). Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at [Delft University of Technology](https://www.tudelft.nl) and the [Amsterdam Institute for Advanced Metropolitan Solutions (AMS)](https://www.ams-institute.org).

My research sits at the intersection of **Human-Computer Interaction** and **Data Management**. I investigate how to integrate human perspectives and computational methods in the design, development, and deployment of data-intensive applications — with a particular focus on healthcare and civic contexts. My goal is to build systems that are not just efficient, but that are responsive to human characteristics, needs, and values.

My current work spans three main directions:

- **Human-Centric Graph Data Management** — interactive and user-driven approaches to graph database repair, query formulation, and causal analysis, in collaboration with the [LIRIS Database Group](https://liris.cnrs.fr).
- **Empathy-Centric Design** — tools and methods for embedding empathy into participatory design processes and policy-making (see the [COCTEAU](https://cocteau-tool.github.io) project).
- **Crowdsourcing and Human-in-the-Loop Systems** — quality-aware human-in-the-loop algorithms for data collection, integration, and analysis.

I hold a PhD from [Politecnico di Milano](https://www.polimi.it), where I developed methodologies for crowd and social-based application design.

I am always looking for motivated **PhD students and interns**. If you are interested in working on human-computer interaction, data management, or their intersection, feel free to get in touch.
